- **2026-09-13** Stage 6 stats + persistence done. Engine gained a `flagsPlaced` counter (snapshot-backed, so undo cannot fake a no-flag run). New `src/game/stats.ts`: headless and pure. Per-mode-per-difficulty `ModeStats` (games/wins/losses, best time, average win time, best Rush score, cells revealed, mines found, flags placed, perfect games, current + best streaks) via `statsKey(mode, bucket)`; `recordRun(...)` is a pure reducer returning stats + achievements + daily history + recent games + `recordBeaten` + `unlocked` in one pass. Records are competitive-gated (`modeDef.competitive`); time records are skipped for Zen/No Guess and Rush scores are tracked instead of time for Rush. Eight real achievements: First Clear, Pure Logic (10 No Guess wins), No Flagger, Speed Demon (Expert < 2:00), Perfect Run, Daily Habit (3-day streak), Zen Mind (5 wins), Rush Hour (1500+). Persistence is corrupt-safe: `loadPersistent`/`savePersistent` with per-validator checks and `loadAll` replaces only the corrupted slice (`swm.stats.v1`), never wiping unrelated data. `src/game/useStatsStore.ts` adds a tiny Zustand surface (`outcomeFor` builds a `RunOutcome` from the engine, `recordFinished` is content-addressed so StrictMode cannot double-count) plus `aggregateStat` for views.
